Dev Toolkit for Odoo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026  |  Developer: Golam Mostafa

In short:

  • The extension works entirely inside your browser.
  • It does not collect, sell or send your data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Screenshots, recordings and settings stay on your device until you download or delete them.

2. What the extension does

The extension helps Odoo developers with:

  • switching Odoo debug modes
  • simulating barcode scans
  • inspecting OWL components in DevTools
  • taking and annotating screenshots
  • recording the screen
  • showing Odoo record details
  • copying Odoo technical field details, exporting field lists, building domains and comparing records
  • inspecting the technologies, performance, SEO and security headers of other websites
  • optionally, showing your own Claude plan usage

What the extension never does

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling or creditworthiness purposes.
  • It does not read, store or transmit passwords, cookies or login tokens.
  • It does not load or run remote code.
  • It does not include analytics or tracking.

4. Screen recording and microphone

  • Recording starts only after you click Start recording.
  • Screen, window and tab sharing always goes through Chrome's own picker or indicator.
  • The microphone is used only while a recording is running, and only after you allow it in Chrome.
  • Recordings are saved in your browser and leave your device only if you download and share them yourself.

5. Claude Usage meter (optional)

This feature is off until you click Show Claude usage and allow access to claude.ai in Chrome's permission prompt.

  • What it reads: the usage percentages and reset times shown on claude.ai's Settings > Usage page, using the session you are already signed in with.
  • Where the data goes: the request goes only from your browser to claude.ai. The values are shown in the popup and cached on your device.
  • Turning it off: click Hide Claude usage to delete the cached values and remove the claude.ai permission.

Your use of claude.ai is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Only the latest screenshot and the latest recording are stored. Each new capture replaces the previous one.
  • Copy history keeps at most 100 values and can be cleared in the Dev tab.
  • You can delete all extension data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome (chrome://extensions).
  • You can also clear it through Chrome's site data settings.
